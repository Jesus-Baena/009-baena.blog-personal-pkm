Deployed to Djibouti during the Horn of Africa crisis to conduct a **exploratory mission**. The objective was to assess humanitarian needs and identify potential WASH projects for future intervention, providing the strategic groundwork for subsequent aid programs.

- Conducted a rapid, in-depth **needs assessment** in vulnerable communities to identify critical gaps in WASH services.
- **Evaluated the feasibility** of potential project sites and intervention strategies.
